# D3-PSM: Proximity Sensor Monitoring

**MITRE D3FEND countermeasure**

## What it does

Monitoring events from proximity sensors that indicate a credential or tagged asset is within the sensor’s read range or a defined zone. Common enabling technologies include RFID, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), and Ultra-Wideband (UWB).

## Attacks this counters

No ATT&CK technique in this corpus maps to this countermeasure. D3FEND may map it to techniques outside the Enterprise matrix, or to ones MITRE has since revoked — absence here is about this corpus, not about the countermeasure.
