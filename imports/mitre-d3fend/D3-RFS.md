# D3-RFS: RF Shielding

**MITRE D3FEND countermeasure**

## What it does

Adding physical barriers to a platform to prevent undesired radio interference.

## Attacks this counters

No ATT&CK technique in this corpus maps to this countermeasure. D3FEND may map it to techniques outside the Enterprise matrix, or to ones MITRE has since revoked — absence here is about this corpus, not about the countermeasure.
